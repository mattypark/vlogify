your clips

You own what you record. By posting a clip into a room you give the members of that room the right to watch it and to include it in the room’s stitched day, which any member may save and share. Our server keeps a clip only long enough to deliver it, and deletes its copy after seven days; the phones in the room keep theirs.

your notes

Notes you write on a day or a room stay on your device. We cannot read them, recover them, or move them to a new phone for you — that is your iCloud backup's job.
