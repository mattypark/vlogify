most of a day never gets filmed.

vlogify catches it one hour at a time.

free on iPhone · six languages · no ads, ever

Start a room with a few friends. Once an hour, everyone in it gets the same nudge at the same minute. Each of you films two seconds of whatever is in front of you: one take, no redo, nothing from the camera roll. At night the clips line up side by side into one vlog you keep.

You draw your own mark when you join. It signs every clip you take, so a room of friends reads like one page of a sketchbook.

how a room works

  1. Tap + and fill in the sentence: a room called something, for you and a few friends. Send them the code.
  2. Each hour the room gets a nudge. Open vlogify, tap the red button, two seconds.
  3. Missed an hour? Your square shows your mark instead. That is a life, not an error.
  4. At night, play the day, save it to Photos, post it wherever you like.

A room just for you has no limits. Days turn over at 4 am.
