A private room of friends all get the same ping once an hour. Everyone shoots one two-second clip — one take, no retakes, nothing from the camera roll. At the end of the day vlogify stitches every clip, side by side, into one video you can save and post anywhere.

Solo vlogs have no limits. The day resets at 4 AM.

how a room works

  1. Tap +, make a log, pick 2–20 people. Share the six-character code.
  2. Every hour, everyone in the room gets the same nudge. Open the app, tap the smiley, two seconds.
  3. Missed an hour? That tile stays empty. It is a life, not an error.
  4. Tap play the day. Save it to Photos. Post it to Reels, TikTok, Shorts.
