privacy policy

effective 11 September 2026

vlogify is a small app with a simple deal: your clips are seen by the people in your room and nobody else. This page says exactly what we hold, why, and how to make it go away.

what we collect

  • Account identifier. Created when you sign in with Apple, Google, or an email code. Used to know which rooms are yours.
  • Display name. The name you type at setup. Shown on your tiles to your room. Sign in with Apple’s name is only used to prefill it.
  • Email address. Only if you sign in by email, and only to deliver the code.
  • Clips. The two-second videos you post, with the room, day and hour they belong to. Stored so the people in your room can watch them and so your day can be stitched.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report a clip or block someone, we keep that so it sticks.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera roll, or analytics. There is no advertising and no tracking, and the app’s privacy manifest says the same thing.

who can see what

Clips are visible only to members of the room they were posted in. Room owners can remove members; anyone can leave. Blocking someone hides their clips from you and yours from them everywhere.

where it lives

Clips and account data are stored with our hosting providers (Supabase for account and room data, Cloudflare R2 for video) in encrypted storage, transferred over HTTPS, and never shared with anyone else except as required by law.

deleting your data

Profile → delete account removes your account, every clip you posted, the rooms you own, and revokes your Sign in with Apple token. It cannot be undone. If you cannot open the app, email hello@vlogify.app from the address on the account and we will do it within 30 days.

children

vlogify is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before it applies.

Questions: hello@vlogify.app
